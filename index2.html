01 / How it works

An agent with a leash made of code.

01

Create your vault

A personal smart-contract vault, owned by your wallet. Fund it with the assets you choose. Only you can withdraw.

02

Set the mandate

Max trade size, daily loss limit, slippage cap and allowed pairs. They live in the contract, not in a database.

03

The agent hunts spreads

It scans HSKSwap for arbitrage and price gaps and proposes swaps. The vault approves each one or reverts it.

04

Reports in plain language

“I bought 1,200 HSK on Pool A, sold on Pool B, net +0.4%.” Talk to it by text or voice. Chat can propose rules, but it can never move money.

03 / Talk to it

Conversation proposes. You confirm.

Text or voice, the agent can only turn your words into a rule-change card. Nothing changes until you confirm, and no message can ever move funds. Confirm a card and watch the mandate above update.
